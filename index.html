The first account · Fragment 001

The world behind the world.

Before the first kingdom raised its walls, the Veil was woven between what is seen and what is not. It was made to keep two worlds apart, not because one was evil, but because both were unfinished.

Beyond it lies Nocturne: a realm shaped by memory, moonlight, and the things people leave unsaid. Its forests listen. Its rivers remember. And somewhere, an old archive keeps a record of every soul that has crossed.

“The night does not conceal the truth. It gives the truth somewhere to hide.”
